Would you like to learn the basics or not-so-basics of Modular synthesis and Eurorack?

Come to an informal Modular Learning session - or invite Simon to give a workshop!

A typical session is interactive and lasts two hours. We cover everything from basic sound physics to analogue oscillators and filters to programming for hardware sequencers.

Everyone learns something 🙂

## Modular synthesis and Eurorack Modules

Exact topics covered vary depending on the group knowledge and interests!

Introduction to modular synthesis

Voltage Controlled Oscillators (VCO)

Voltage Controlled Amplifiers (VCA)

Voltage Controlled Filters (VCF)

Sequencers

Slew limiters

Quantisers

Precision Adders

Mults

Mixers

Oscilloscopes

Logic gates

Clocks

Clock Dividers

Clock Multipliers

Midi integration

Offset and Gain

Trigger Delays

Switches

Sequential Switches

### Programming for Eurorack

These sessions provide an introduction to coding for Eurorack modules such as:

Bela

Teensy

Norns

Raspberry Pi

using C++, Lua and Rust
