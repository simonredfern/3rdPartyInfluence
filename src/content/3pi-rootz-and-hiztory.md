3rd Party Influence started life when Simon Redfern and Torsten Müller met in New Zealand at the University of Aukland computer music lab circa 2002 and they started messing around with drum machines and synths. Some verrrry lucky parties and venues had the pleazure of their sonic company.

Martin Gordon and Daniel Tippmann joined for several events in Berlin.

Simon and Daniel perform regularly these days at [Berlin Modular Society](http://www.berlinmodularsociety.com) and other events and sometimes Simon plays solo sets.
