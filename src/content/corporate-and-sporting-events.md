Whilst we normally perform at clubs and parties, we sometimes play at corporate, sporting and education events too!

Need the music to be a bit slower, quieter more mellow? - no problem. Need us to play all night to keep a bunch of endurance cyclists going? - no problem. What to get your hard working colleagues, who maybe meet once a year, dancing? - no problem. Want to involve some data visualization, live visuals or other audience interactions? - no problem.

Want to get a short introduction into modular with perhaps some nerdy tech topics (APIs, JSON, UDP etc) thrown in? Sure! CO2e data? Absolutely!

Contact us [directly on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/3rdpartyinfluence/) or via [Berlin Modular Society](https://www.berlinmodularsociety.com), where we can also involve other / alternative artists to meet your specific requirements. For educational workshops see [here](/modular-learning)
